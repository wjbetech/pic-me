# Modal art direction — professional and modern, matching the app

Status: resolved

## Answer

Modal mirrors the homepage's sticker-book language: `font-display` on "Leave this game?", `border-4 border-base-content` flat cards, scrim stays `bg-black/50`, actions are sticker buttons (Home = `bg-accent`, Settings = `bg-base-100` with border). One aesthetic risk, justified.
